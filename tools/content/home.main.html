Questions

Before you book.

How much will my bathroom cost?

Our full bathroom remodels range from $16,000 to $28,500. Larger or more complex projects may exceed this range. Your exact price depends on the size, the layout, your materials and what's behind the old walls. You get it in writing within 5 hours after a free visit.Smaller bathrooms fall at the lower end. Price depends on scope. Plus WA sales tax.

How long does it take?

Typically 2–4 weeks of construction, depending on scope and what we find. You get the start date in writing.

What if you find damage behind the wall?

We stop, show you photos, and give you a written price. Nothing happens without your written OK.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When your project includes plumbing or electrical work, we do it with a permit and handle the paperwork.

Who does the remodeling work?

Serhii measures, writes your price and stays your one point of contact. The remodeling work is done by our crew. One contract, one price, one warranty.

How do payments work?

You pay a deposit when you sign the contract, the next payment after the rough-in inspection passes, and the final payment after our walkthrough together. The visit and the written price are free.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. 1-year written workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials.

What happens after I request a visit?

Serhii calls you from (206) 222-2328 to arrange a time. He measures your bathroom at your home, and you get your price in writing within 5 hours after the visit.

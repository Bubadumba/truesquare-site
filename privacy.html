6. Text Messaging (SMS) Terms & Opt-In Compliance

We value your privacy and comply strictly with A2P 10DLC cellular carrier standards and TCPA regulations.

Optional SMS Consent: Opting in to receive text messages is completely voluntary. SMS consent is collected via an explicit, separate, unchecked checkbox on our website forms. Consent is never pre-selected and is never a condition of service or purchase.

Message Scope: We send only transactional and conversational text messages directly related to your inquiry, estimate request, appointment reminders, and active project updates. We do not send automated marketing text blasts.

Frequency & Rates: Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and project activity. Standard message and data rates may apply from your wireless provider.

Opt-Out & Help: You may opt out of text messaging at any time by replying STOP to any text message. For assistance, reply HELP, call or text us at (206) 222-2328, or email info@truesquareremodel.com.

What changes

A new shower in the same spot.

A shower remodel keeps the shower where it is and replaces what wore out: the tile, the grout, the valve, the door. It is a good fit when the rest of the bathroom is fine.

We take the old shower down to the framing, check what is behind it, and build it back with fresh tile and fixtures. If we find damage, you get photos and a written price first.

  • Old tile and surround outDown to the framing so we can see what is behind it.
  • New tile and nicheWalls and floor in the tile you choose.
  • Valve, shower head and trimPlumbing work is done with a permit.
  • Glass door or panelSliding, hinged or fixed panel.

Questions

Shower remodel questions.

Can you remodel just the shower?

Yes. We can rebuild only the shower, or the whole bathroom. Your written scope shows exactly what is included.

How long does a shower remodel take?

Most bathroom projects are completed in 1–3 weeks once work starts. Your start date is in writing before you sign.

My shower leaks behind the tile. Can you fix that?

We open it up, show you what we find with photos, and give you a written price for the fix before we continue.

Do you replace the shower valve?

Yes, when your project needs it. Plumbing work is done with a permit.

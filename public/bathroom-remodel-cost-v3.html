Straight answers

Ask Us the Awkward Questions.

Will the price jump once the walls are open?

Only if we find hidden damage or you change the scope. We stop, show you photos, and give you a written price. Nothing happens without your written OK.

Why can't you price it over the phone?

We need to see and measure the room first. The $16,000–$28,500 range is not a quote for your home, and WA sales tax is extra. Your written price follows within 5 hours after the free visit.

Are you the one swinging the hammer?

No. Our subcontractor crew does the construction. Serhii coordinates the project and is your phone/text contact.

Do I pay everything up front?

No. You pay a deposit at signing, another payment after the rough-in inspection, and the final payment after the walkthrough.

How long will I be without a bathroom?

Typically 1–3 weeks of construction, depending on scope and what we find. You get the start date in writing.

What if something fails after you leave?

You get a 1-year written workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials.

Questions

Before you book.

How much will my bathroom cost?

Industry data for the Seattle area puts most bathroom remodels between about $15,000 and $36,000 (Angi 2026 average; Remodeling Cost vs. Value 2025, mid-range bath). It depends on the size of the room, what changes (tile only, or the tub, layout and plumbing too) and the tile and fixtures you pick. Any number over the phone is a guess. After the free visit, you get your price in writing within 5 hours.

How long will the remodel take?

Most bathrooms are completed in 1–3 weeks once work starts. You get the start date in writing before you sign.

What if you find hidden damage?

If we find rot, mold or old work that isn't up to code, we stop, send you photos and a written price for the fix. Nothing moves forward without your OK. The rest of your price stays the same.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When your project includes plumbing or electrical work, we do it with a permit and handle the paperwork.

Who does the remodeling work?

Serhii measures, writes your price and stays your one point of contact. The remodeling work is done by our crew. One contract, one price, one warranty.

How do payments work?

You pay a deposit when you sign the contract, the next payment after the rough-in inspection passes, and the final payment after our walkthrough together. The visit and the written price are free.

What's the warranty?

1 year on our workmanship, plus the manufacturer's warranty on fixtures and materials.

What happens after I request a visit?

Serhii calls you from (206) 222-2328 to arrange a time. He measures your bathroom at your home, and you get your price in writing within 5 hours after the visit.

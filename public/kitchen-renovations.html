What we do

A kitchen you know the price of.

Questions

Kitchen questions.

Do you remodel kitchens or only bathrooms?

Most of our work is bathrooms, and we also remodel kitchens: cabinets, countertops, backsplash and fixtures.

Can you replace only the countertops or backsplash?

Tell us what you have in mind. Serhii will tell you on the call if it is a fit, and the written price lists exactly what is included.

Do you handle electrical and plumbing in the kitchen?

Yes. When your project includes plumbing or electrical work, we do it with a permit.

What if you find a problem behind the old cabinets?

We stop, send you photos and a written price for the fix. Nothing moves forward without your OK.

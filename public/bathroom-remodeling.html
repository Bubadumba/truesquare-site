What it means

A full remodel, start to finish.

A full bathroom remodel means the old room comes out down to the studs or subfloor where needed, and a new bathroom goes in. You choose what changes: the tile, the tub or shower, the vanity, the toilet, the lighting.

Serhii comes to your home, measures, and talks through what you want. Then you get a written scope of work and one price. You know what is included before anything starts.

Our crew does the remodeling work. Serhii stays your one point of contact from the first visit to the final walk-through.

  • Vanity, toilet and fixturesFaucets, shower valve and trim, mirror, accessories.

Questions

Full remodel questions.

How long does a full bathroom remodel take?

Most bathrooms are completed in 1–3 weeks once work starts. The exact start date is in your written agreement before you sign.

Can I keep my layout?

Yes. You decide. Keeping the toilet, tub or shower where they are is usually the simpler option. Moving plumbing is possible and is done with a permit.

What if you find damage behind the old tile?

We stop, send you photos and a written price for the fix. Nothing moves forward without your OK. The rest of your price stays the same.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When your remodel includes plumbing or electrical work, we do it with a permit and handle the paperwork.

Can I see how the work is going?

Yes. If you want them, you get progress photos every day.

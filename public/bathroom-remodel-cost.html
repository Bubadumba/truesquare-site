Questions

Common Questions

Can you give me a price before the visit?

Our full remodels range from $16,000–$28,500. Smaller bathrooms fall at the lower end. Larger or more complex projects may exceed this range. Price depends on scope. Plus WA sales tax. An exact price needs a visit, because the layout and what's behind the walls change the number. You get it in writing within 5 hours after the visit.

What if you find damage behind the wall?

We stop, show you photos, and give you a written price. Nothing happens without your written OK.

How long does it take?

Typically 2–4 weeks of construction, depending on scope and what we find. You get the start date in writing.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. 1-year written workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer warranties on fixtures and materials.

Who will I deal with?

Serhii is your phone/text contact from the first visit to the final walkthrough. Our subcontractor crew does the work. Any extra work needs your written OK first.

How do payments work?

You pay a deposit when you sign the contract, the next payment after the rough-in inspection passes, and the final payment after our walkthrough together. The visit and the written price are free.

Do you do small repairs?

We focus on full bathroom and shower remodels. Small repairs only when our schedule allows.

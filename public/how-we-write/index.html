About the blog

How we write these guides.

What goes into every article before it is published.

Every guide on this site is written for homeowners in Seattle and nearby cities. Prices come from our own estimates and written subcontractor quotes, not from national averages. Permit, tax and code facts are checked against the official city, county and state pages we link to, with the month we checked them. Drafts are prepared with AI tools from our job notes and sources, then read, corrected and approved by Serhii Tomilov before they are published. If you find a mistake, email info@truesquareremodel.com.

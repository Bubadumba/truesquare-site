Where we tile

Tile is most of what you see.

In a bathroom, tile is the first thing you notice and the first thing that shows a mistake. Flat floors, even grout lines and clean edges at niches and corners matter.

Most of our tile work is part of a bathroom or kitchen remodel. We also take on tile projects on their own: a new bathroom floor, a backsplash or a fireplace surround.

  • Bathroom and laundry floorsPorcelain, mosaic and hexagon tile.
  • Shower walls and floorsIncluding niches and lit niches.
  • Tub surroundsSubway, large-format and stone-look tile.
  • Kitchen backsplashesFrom subway tile to slab look.
  • Fireplace surroundsTile and stacked stone.

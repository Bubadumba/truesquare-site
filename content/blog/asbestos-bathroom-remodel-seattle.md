Check for asbestos ahead of demolition day. Two agencies have rules. The Puget Sound Clean Air Agency covers air and notices. Washington L&I covers the inspection that comes first and the workers.

In a bathroom, the usual suspect is old sheet vinyl flooring. This page explains what each agency says, in plain words. We read their pages in November 2026.

## Do I need an asbestos survey for a bathroom remodel in Seattle?

Yes. Someone has to check the materials the work will disturb. L&I says owners and contractors must do this ahead of any remodel, and that an accredited inspector, such as an AHERA Building Inspector, performs it ([Washington L&I, checked November 2026](https://www.lni.wa.gov/safety-health/safety-topics/topics/asbestos)).

The Puget Sound Clean Air Agency covers Seattle and the rest of King County. Its homeowner page lists the survey as step one ([PSCAA, checked November 2026](https://www.pscleanair.gov/192/Homeowner-Renovation)).

## Can I survey my own house?

Sometimes. If you own and live in a single-family house, PSCAA says you may do the survey for your own renovation. Otherwise it says you must hire an AHERA-certified building inspector. A homeowner cannot self-survey for demolishing the house itself ([PSCAA FAQ, checked November 2026](https://pscleanair.gov/m/faq?cat=20)).

L&I points the same way. It says owners of a non-commercial home are exempt from the general survey requirements. They still have to pass what they know to any workers they hire (L&I, checked November 2026).

Our advice: if you hire a contractor, ask at the first visit who orders the survey and who pays for it. Put the answer in the written scope.

## Is sheet vinyl in my bathroom asbestos?

You cannot tell by looking. A lab or an accredited inspector decides. What PSCAA does say is which materials to worry about. It names sheet vinyl flooring with a paper-like backing as an example of friable asbestos-containing material. Friable means it crumbles or turns to powder by hand pressure, so fibers can get into the air. It lists vinyl asbestos tile, mastic and caulk as materials that may be nonfriable, depending on their condition ([PSCAA FAQ, checked November 2026](https://pscleanair.gov/m/faq?cat=20)).

That difference matters for your plan. A tile that stays intact is handled differently from a backing that tears into pieces during demolition. In a 1960s Seattle bathroom, the floor is the first place we would look.

## What notice do I file, and what does it cost?

Before you remove friable asbestos from your own single-family home, PSCAA wants a Single-Family Residence Notification, with a $25.00 filing fee. Keep a printed copy on site. Debris has to be disposed of within 10 days ([PSCAA, checked November 2026](https://www.pscleanair.gov/192/Homeowner-Renovation)).

That is the homeowner route. We could not verify the notice and fee rules for a contractor-run job, so we do not quote them here. Call PSCAA or ask the certified contractor who files.

## Who is allowed to remove it?

It depends on who owns and lives in the house. PSCAA says owners of an occupied single-family house may remove it themselves. Renters, owners of rental property and condo owners must hire a certified asbestos abatement contractor.

L&I adds that contractors and workers must be trained and certified to work with asbestos. A general remodeler is not that. If your floor tests positive and has to come out, expect a separate certified crew to do that part before the remodel starts.

## What does it cost to skip the survey?

L&I says you can be fined $600 a day, and both contractors and building owners can be fined ([Washington L&I, checked November 2026](https://www.lni.wa.gov/safety-health/safety-topics/topics/asbestos)). PSCAA also mentions notices of violation and monetary penalties, but gives no amount.

There is a second cost, and it is time. Asbestos found halfway through demolition stops the job.

## Does Renton add anything?

Renton's permit page says an asbestos survey of the work area is required before renovation or demolition, and that it follows PSCAA and L&I rules ([City of Renton, checked November 2026](https://rentonwa.gov/City-Services/Permit-Center/Residential-Interior-Remodels)). :::faq
- q: Does a 1990s house need an asbestos survey before a bathroom remodel?
  a: L&I's rule applies before any remodel, whatever the age of the house. The survey checks the materials the work will disturb. A newer house will usually have fewer suspect materials, but the inspector decides, not the year on the deed.
- q: Can I remove old sheet vinyl myself?
  a: If you own and live in a single-family house, PSCAA says you may. You file the Single-Family Residence Notification with a $25.00 fee first. Renters, rental owners and condo owners must hire a certified abatement contractor instead.
- q: Who can do the survey?
  a: An accredited inspector such as an AHERA Building Inspector. PSCAA lets an owner-occupant of a single-family house survey their own renovation. It does not allow a self-survey for demolishing the house.
- q: What is friable asbestos?
  a: Material that crumbles or turns to powder by hand pressure. PSCAA gives sheet vinyl with a paper-like backing as an example. Friable material releases fibers easily, so removing it has more rules than leaving it intact.
- q: What fine applies if the inspection is skipped?
  a: L&I says $600 a day, and contractors and building owners can both be fined. PSCAA mentions notices of violation and penalties but publishes no amount on the page we read.
:::

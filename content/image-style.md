# Image style for generated illustrations (read before every generation)
Purpose: illustrate a bathroom or kitchen idea, a material, a layout, a procedure or a diagram. Never a specific TrueSquare job, never a client's home, never a before/after pair.
Write the prompt as a brief, in this order: scene -> subject -> details with dimensions -> how it was captured -> constraints.
Capture description, always included: "real photograph, shot on an iPhone, flat overcast daylight from a window, eye level, candid, no retouching, slight natural lens distortion, realistic grout and caulk lines". Name materials with dimensions (12x24 inch porcelain tile, 36 inch vanity, 1/16 inch grout joint, 60 inch alcove tub). Seattle cues where natural: 1950s-60s house, 5x8 foot bathroom, wood-framed window, rain on the glass, gray daylight.
Banned words in prompts: 8K, 4K, hyperrealistic, photorealistic, cinematic, masterpiece, stunning, beautiful, luxury, golden hour, dramatic lighting, HDR, Portra, flawless, award-winning.
Never in the image: people, faces, hands, pets, text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names, watermarks, our logo, a street or house exterior, a before/after split, a room that copies a photo from photos.json.
Composition: one subject and one idea per image; straight verticals; the detail the paragraph talks about in the center third; nothing cropped awkwardly. Hero 3:2 landscape. In-body detail 4:3 or 1:1. Diagram 16:9.
Diagrams and schemes: flat vector look, two colors plus gray, white background, labels only with words that already exist in the article (spell each label in quotes and ask to render it exactly once), no decorative icons, no shadows.
Reject a candidate when: tile joints do not line up or grout lines bend; plumbing is impossible (drain in a wall, faucet without handles, two shower heads facing each other, a toilet with no supply line); doors or windows open into nothing; a mirror reflects a different room; any text or letters appear; the room has no plausible exit; proportions are wrong; the surface looks like plastic or a 3D render; the image repeats a composition already used on the site.
